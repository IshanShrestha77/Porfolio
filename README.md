## The Site
Built around a robotics + aviation theme. The hero opens with a plane actually landing on a runway before my name fades in — that's the part I'm proudest of. From there it covers who I am, what drives me (robotics, coding, aviation, innovation), my project log (Rakshak 2.0 and Skyro), my toolbox, achievements, journey, and goals — all tied together with an aviation "cockpit" look, down to the contact form, which actually emails me when someone sends a message.

## Use of AI 

In my work, I used an AI (Claude) as a coding assistant. I did not use it for designing the website or developing the concept (robotics + aviation, plane-landing opening scene, contact form as cockpit, and the visual language of the instruments). However, I used AI for designing the plane-landing animation (the scene with the plane approaching the airport),JavaScript assistance (writing the code itself, debugging),formspree installation,andhelping me realize how to implement my ideas (turning them into the actual code)


The reason for using AI for these particular tasks was :
1) my lack of experience with coding. Even though I tried to learn JavaScript on my own and had some general understanding of what I needed to do (e.g., create an SVG of an aircraft and animate it with GSAP), I needed assistance when it came to actual writing of code. I could not always predict the exact syntax or know all the nuances of working with GSAP, which led to errors on my part (e.g., in the soundOn/soundoff functionality, I had a typo in one of the variables that I could not spot right away). 

2) I did not know how to set up the contact form to send me an e-mail (not open the e-mail client on the user’s device) either, and I needed help understanding what Formspree was and how it worked. 

3) Finally, since my knowledge of JavaScript was superficial and recent, I relied on AI to help me visualize how turn my ideas into actual code. I did not use AI to design the website, however – the concepts and ideas were mine, and I used AI to assist me in writing code, which I did not know how to write yet.
